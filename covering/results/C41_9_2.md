## Two ways this one differs from the others

**Its published value was genuinely searched.** The cell's entire history is a
single line - 28 blocks by simulated annealing, Colin Barker, 2008-11-28. This
is not an inherited value restricted from a neighbour; somebody pointed a real
search at these exact parameters and got 28. It is the first record here that
beats a number someone actually worked for.

**It was found by the algebra, not by free search.** The design is base blocks
developed under a group of order 3 acting in thirteen 3-cycles, with two points
left fixed. Every earlier record came from unstructured restarts, and the
orbit machinery had until now produced nothing but oversized designs. On this
cell it is what worked.

The group is necessarily non-transitive: 27 blocks of 9 points is 243
incidences over 41 points, or 5.93 each, so the degrees cannot be constant -
and indeed 38 points lie in 6 blocks and 3 points in 5.
